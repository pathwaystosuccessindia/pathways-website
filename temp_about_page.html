Our inspiration

We decided to start this non-profit organisation having seen the profound lack of resources in a school very close to our grandmother’s house in Coorg, India. She completed her PhD at a time when women faced adversity when pursuing higher education which led her to champion the access to education for all, regardless of socioeconomic or gender barriers. After seeing her strive for the education of all throughout her life, we decided to continue our grandmother’s legacy by starting this non-profit to help children in need. We’ve been privileged to have easy access to a great education in schools with excellent resources and opportunities and would love to be able to provide a similar pathway to success for underprivileged children and low-income families in India. 

Though we did not have the opportunity to get to know our grandfather, this mission was equally important to him and we hope to make them both proud.

Dr Nirmala Somaiah
