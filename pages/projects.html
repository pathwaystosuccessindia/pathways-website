Our Project Areas

Seven key focus areas driving educational transformation across India

Featured

Virtual Learning Sessions

Through live Zoom sessions, we connect directly with students in the schools we support to explore important topics that go beyond academics — such as the impact of screen time, the benefits of staying physically active, and building healthy everyday habits. Each session is interactive and relatable, giving students practical takeaways they can apply in their daily lives.

Active

Technology

Our technology initiative brings computers to underprivileged children, giving them access to essential digital tools. We also aim to set up a teaching program to foster computer literacy. In a world increasingly reliant on technology, it is vital that all children have access to good technology that allows them to gain the invaluable skills needed to excel in a workplace.

Active

Classroom Resources

Many students in India lack key learning equipment such as books, stationary, textbooks and bags that are vital to giving them an enriching learning experience. Often due to lack of school funding and parents not being able to afford new equipment for their children, students are forced to learn with old and poor quality resources.

We aim to deliver new resources to these schools including reading books for all ages, notebooks, textbook sets for each classroom, stationary and more.

Active

Health Education

Many schools do not adequately teach children about proper nutrition or essential hygiene practices, leaving gaps in their education. We are dedicated to helping promote the overall-wellbeing of the children we serve by teaching the importance of eating healthy, well-balanced meals and regular exercise. Additionally, we emphasize hygiene practices especially for young girls, to ensure they have the knowledge to take care of their bodies.

Active

Sports

Sports can be such an amazing outlet for so many students and we are very grateful to have access to and be able to try many different types of sports. Unfortunately, although there are many budding athletes in these schools, their equipment is often limited and in need of renewal.

We would like to deliver a range of new equipment to these schools so that the students can enjoy and try their hand at a variety of different sports.

Active

Scholarships and Grants

Many talented and hardworking students across India are unable to pursue higher education due to financial barriers. Despite their potential, the cost of tuition, books, uniforms, and transportation often puts continued education out of reach for families already struggling to meet daily needs.

Through our scholarship initiative, we aim to support these students by covering essential educational expenses, helping them stay in school and strive toward brighter futures. Our goal is to identify deserving students and provide them with the financial assistance they need to complete their schooling and access opportunities for higher education.

Active

Learning Programs

Alongside, and due to, poor technological equipment, many students in India lack basic computer literacy skills and proper resources for learning to code and other general computer skills. Lots of students will also choose not to pursue higher education for many different reasons including financial reasons and family responsibilities.

In our education programs we aim to teach young kids basic coding and computer literacy in order to open them up to the wide world of computer science and prepare them for any roles technology might have in their careers in later life. We will tailor the sessions based on age groups and we aim to teach them basic coding with resources such as Scratch and we will teach them how to stay safe online. We also hope to run college and careers advice sessions in which we will show students the benefits of higher education and encourage them to think about their futures as young scholars.
