About the School

Gen. Thimmayya School in Madikeri, Kodagu serves as a vital educational institution in the Karnataka region. Through our partnership with this school, we're working to provide quality education and essential resources to students from underserved communities.

Named after General K. S. Thimayya, one of Kodagu's most distinguished sons, the school nurtures well-rounded students through academics, cultural events, sports, and uniformed youth programs such as Scouts and NCC.
