Give a Little, Feel a Lot

Lighting the Path to Meaningful Education One Child and One School at a Time

As part of Mira's Eagle Scout Project, working with Fields of Dreams Uganda, we saw firsthand how even a small effort can create lasting change. Seeing the smiles of children and feeling their gratitude for something as simple as the chance to attend school and learn computer literacy filled us with an indescribable joy — the kind that stays with you long after the moment has passed.

That experience inspired us to start Pathways to Success India — in memory of our grandmother, who believed that education is the greatest path to elevating oneself. She devoted her life to helping children learn, no matter their circumstances.

Today, we continue her dream by supporting under-resourced schools in India, where even a modest contribution can make a meaningful difference.
