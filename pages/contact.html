Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

How can I volunteer with your organization?

We welcome volunteers! Fill out the contact form above selecting "Volunteering" as your interest, or email us directly at pathwaystosuccessindia@gmail.com with your skills, availability, and areas of interest.

Are donations tax-deductible?

Yes! We are a registered 501(c)(3) non-profit organization. All donations are tax-deductible to the full extent allowed by law. You'll receive a receipt immediately after donating.

How can my school apply for support?

Schools can apply for support by contacting us through the form above, selecting "School Support Request." Please include information about your school, student demographics, current needs, and how our programs could help.

Do you accept corporate partnerships?

Absolutely! We partner with corporations for CSR initiatives, employee engagement programs, and long-term educational projects. Contact us to discuss partnership opportunities.

How can I track the impact of my donation?

We provide regular updates to our donors through email newsletters, impact reports, and social media. You'll see exactly how your contribution is making a difference in children's lives.
